Academic Risk Model Architecture
ACRS_s = (0.40 × NIFS_s) + (0.35 × WSS_s) + (0.25 × NSCS_s)

ACRS (Academic Cyber Risk Score): Evaluates sector risk on a strict [0, 100] scale combining relative incident volume (NIFS), weighted incident severity (WSS), and systemic dependency criticality (NSCS).

NCRI = Σ (ACRS_s × SCW_s) for all evaluated critical sectors s

NCRI (National Cyber Risk Index): Weighted national composite index. Sum of sector criticality weights equals 1.00 (excluding unclassified entities).
